Step by step

The warmup

  1. Day 1Create

    Create your accounts

    Make a new account on each of these apps:

    TikTokInstagramYouTubeFacebook

    Use a username that combines your name with one of these:

    leanwith_hackswfitwgrubw_xfit_eats_fuel_gainz_getsleanwhat_eatshealthw_
  2. Days 2–3Warm up

    Train the algorithm Important

    This is your bulletproof excuse to doomscroll!

    Stay inside the healthy eating out and fitness niche the whole time.

    • Like videos in the niche.
    • Repost 20+ videos from the niche.
    • Follow 20+ creators in the niche.
    • Comment on videos every so often.

    This step is the difference between about 200 views and 1,000+ on your first post. Don't skip it.

  3. Before you uploadProve it

    Send quick screen recordings

    Before your first upload, screen record your TikTok and your Instagram and send both to the MenuFit team on SideShift or Discord. Each recording should show:

    • A few scrolls through your feed (the For You page on TikTok, Reels on Instagram).
    • Your profile, showing you follow 20+ creators.
    • Your reposts, showing 20+ reposted videos.

After warmup

Time to post

Your accounts are warmed up. Here's how to start posting.

  1. Find the 4 formats

    The MenuFit creator site (linked at the top of this page) has 4 video formats to choose from.

  2. Send your first one for review

    The first time you make each format, send the video in your private creator Discord channel or on SideShift for approval before posting it.

  3. Post

    You only need a review once per format. After that, you're good to post that format whenever you're ready.

Expectations

  • Your first post in each format needs approval. Send it in your private creator Discord channel or on SideShift for approval before posting.
  • Say the CTA word for word. The rest is yours. Every script on the creator site ends with a CTA. That part needs to be said exactly as written. Everything before it is a starting point, so put it in your own words and make it sound like you.
    Rest of the scriptCreative freedom
    CTAWord for word
    For your first few days of posting you don't need a hard MenuFit CTA. Focus on getting your account warmed up and building your content style, and the mention can be light. After the first few days, use the exact CTA from the script.
  • 2 or 3 posts a day, cross-posted, depending on your campaign. We know you have a life. If you can't post, just let us know 😌
