MenuFit creator onboarding

Warm up your accounts before you post

New accounts that post right away get very little reach. Spend five days using your new accounts like a normal person who loves food, so TikTok and Instagram learn what your page is about. Then your first MenuFit video lands in front of the right people.

Day 1

Create your accounts

  • Make brand-new accounts on TikTok, Instagram and Facebook. Don't reuse an old or personal page.
  • Pick one username and use it on all three. Combine your name with one of these:
    leanwith_hackswfitwgrubw_xfit_eats_fuel_gainz_getsleanwhat_eatshealthw_
  • Use a clear photo of your face as the profile picture, and your first name as the display name.
  • Write a short food bio, for example "Healthy orders at the places you already eat 🍔🥗". No links yet.
  • Sign up on your own phone with the app, not a computer. Use your normal Wi-Fi or mobile data, and turn any VPN off.

Day by day

The 5-day warmup

Do each day on all three apps. Watch videos to the end, and only like or comment on things you actually enjoy.

  1. Day 1Scroll

    Finish your profile. Scroll the For You / Explore feed for 20–30 minutes. Stop on food videos and skip everything else quickly.

  2. Day 2Follow

    Follow 10–15 creators who post restaurant reviews, fast-food orders and healthy eating. Like 10–20 of their videos. Use search to find them:

    healthy fast food orderhigh protein Chipotlewhat to order atlow calorie restaurantmacro friendly eating out
  3. Day 3Engage

    Like 20–30 food videos, save 5, and leave 5–10 real comments (a full sentence, not just an emoji). Follow 5 more food creators.

  4. Day 4Check

    Keep engaging like day 3. Then check your feed: most of what you see should be food and restaurant content. If it isn't, spend extra time searching and watching food videos today.

  5. Day 5Prep

    One more normal session. Film your first MenuFit video and save it as a draft. Don't post it yet. Send your three handles to the MenuFit team.

  6. Day 6Post

    Post your first video. Keep scrolling and engaging a little every day after this, so your accounts stay active between posts.

Rules

Do this, avoid that

Do

  • Use your own phone and your own SIM or home Wi-Fi.
  • Keep each app logged into only the new account during warmup.
  • Watch videos all the way through.
  • Act like a real fan of food content, at a normal pace.
  • Ask the team if anything about your account looks off.

Don't

  • Post anything before day 6.
  • Mention MenuFit or add a link in your bio until the team says so.
  • Use a VPN, emulator, or automation tool.
  • Follow or unfollow in big bursts (more than ~20 at once).
  • Buy followers or likes, or repost other people's videos.
